# PawsIndia Website Strategic Audit

### Project Summary

Conducted a comprehensive strategic audit of PawsIndia's website, analyzing brand positioning, UX/UI, conversion optimization, trust architecture, SEO, merchandising strategy, and competitive positioning.

### Key Deliverables

* Brand Positioning Analysis
* UX/UI Audit
* Conversion Funnel Audit
* SEO & Content Analysis
* Trust & Credibility Assessment
* Competitive Landscape Review
* Strategic Growth Recommendations
* Prioritized Action Plan

### Key Findings

* Identified multiple conversion blockers affecting purchase decisions.
* Highlighted trust gaps, inconsistent branding, and user experience issues.
* Recommended improvements for homepage conversion, founder visibility, social proof, and customer trust.
* Developed short-term and long-term growth recommendations.
