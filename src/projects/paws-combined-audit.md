## Website Strategic Audit

### Project Summary

Conducted a comprehensive strategic audit of PawsIndia's website, analyzing brand positioning, UX/UI, conversion optimization, trust architecture, SEO, merchandising strategy, and competitive positioning.

### Key Deliverables

- Brand Positioning Analysis
- UX/UI Audit
- Conversion Funnel Audit
- SEO & Content Analysis
- Trust & Credibility Assessment
- Competitive Landscape Review
- Strategic Growth Recommendations
- Prioritized Action Plan

### Key Findings

- Identified multiple conversion blockers affecting purchase decisions.
- Highlighted trust gaps, inconsistent branding, and user experience issues.
- Recommended improvements for homepage conversion, founder visibility, social proof, and customer trust.
- Developed short-term and long-term growth recommendations.

## Instagram Content Strategy Audit

### Project Summary

Performed an in-depth Instagram content and growth audit for PawsIndia, evaluating content pillars, engagement strategy, community building, and content formats.

### Key Deliverables

- Instagram Profile Audit
- Content Strategy Review
- Content Pillar Analysis
- Engagement Assessment
- Community Building Analysis
- Growth Opportunity Mapping
- Content Calendar Recommendations
- Strategic Growth Roadmap

### Key Findings

- Identified strong trend-jacking and reel strategy execution.
- Highlighted missed opportunities in educational content, founder-led content, and community building.
- Recommended content diversification through carousels, UGC programs, and founder storytelling.
- Developed actionable growth recommendations for short-term and long-term audience growth.
